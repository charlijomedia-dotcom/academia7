# Appendices: Contents and Reading Guide

The appendices support the dissertation's main argument and follow the order in which the empirical evidence is tested. They are designed to make reported results verifiable without moving essential model equations, test hypotheses or the main economic conclusions out of Chapters 1–4. The draft pagination of the main text is **not** a scientific constraint. Where a result requires additional tables to be comprehensible and checkable, that evidence remains included.

## Recommended reading sequence

Chapter 2 first defines the forecasting design and retained tests. Appendix A provides detailed observation, transformation and pretest evidence. Chapter 3 then reports model adequacy, predictive accuracy and historical economic-state comparisons; Appendix B supplies the comprehensive tabular information behind that argument. Chapter 4 challenges the baseline evidence through alternative specifications and numerical diagnostics; Appendix C presents the corresponding results. Appendix D provides the rules and evidence necessary to inspect numerical reliability and reproducibility.

The figure responding directly to the earlier implausible unemployment forecasting scale is retained in **Chapter 3, Figure 3.2**, not buried in an appendix. The extreme CPI STAR forecast diagnostic is likewise visible in **Chapter 4, Figure 4.1**. The detailed three-state unemployment probability diagnostic is supplementary **Figure C.1**.

A separate front-matter **List of Abbreviations and Mathematical Notation** is intended to precede Chapter 1; it is not an appendix. The dissertation also needs one consolidated bibliography at final assembly.

These appendices contain selected, published-value extracts rather than arbitrary unreported computations or thousands of individual rolling estimates. A retrospective smoothed regime assignment, a failed forecast, a finite but extreme forecast, a nominally significant test, and an uninformative degenerate test must remain different objects throughout the manuscript.
