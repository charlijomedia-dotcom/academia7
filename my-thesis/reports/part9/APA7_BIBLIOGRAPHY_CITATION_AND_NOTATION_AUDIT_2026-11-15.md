## 5. Mathematical symbols and cross-equation consistency

All **39 displayed numbered equations** were inventoried and inspected against their text definitions: eight in Chapter 1, twenty-one in Chapter 2, four in Chapter 3 and six in Chapter 4. Formula blocks retain matched mathematical environments and the existing equation-number sequence. The following substantive notation issues were corrected:

1. **$K$ collision:** Chapter 2 formerly used $K$ for the Ljung–Box maximum autocorrelation lag even though $K$ denotes the number of latent MSAR regimes in the same methodology. Equation (2.14) and its surrounding chi-square reference description now use **$L$** for test lags; **$K$** remains the number of regimes.
2. **Parameter-vector identity:** In Chapter 2, Equation (2.21) used $\widehat\theta$ for the fitted MSAR vector, potentially confusable with the scalar moving-average coefficients $\theta_j$ and the STAR transition vector $\boldsymbol\theta$. This is now $\widehat\vartheta$; the full-sample conditioning set $y_{1:T}$ is defined.
3. **BIC equation:** The maximized log likelihood $\ell_m(\widehat\vartheta_m)$ and its fitted parameter vector are defined explicitly beside Equation (2.6).
4. **GARCH equation:** The positive variance intercept $\omega$, squared-innovation coefficient $\alpha$, and lagged-variance coefficient $\beta$ are now explained explicitly beside Equation (2.8).
5. **Notation register:** Added missing Fisher, wage, money/velocity, potential-output, Okun, ADF, GARCH, STAR and forecasting-evaluation symbols and explicitly noted equation-specific reuse of Greek letters.

The register now documents **51 mathematical-notation entries**; it is supplementary to the local equation explanations rather than a replacement. All scientific distinctions concerning fitted full-sample parameter equations versus rolling forecasts, smoothed versus real-time state probabilities and statistical versus economic regime interpretations remain intact.
