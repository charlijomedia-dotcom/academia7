## Explicit limitations that must remain unresolved in the scientific text

- **MSAR global likelihood optimum:** The numerical optimization records show local-solution sensitivity. Multiple starting values and converged estimates are *not* proofs of global maximization. Do not mark this as certified or delete the caution in Chapter 4 or the conclusion.
- **Unemployment K=3:** The attained three-state log-likelihood is below the two-state log-likelihood despite the model's greater flexibility. The available two-versus-three-state comparison is **inconclusive**, not a statistical proof that K=2 is superior or structurally correct.
- **Residual adequacy and explosive predictions:** A fitted nonlinear likelihood, including CPI's interpretable state means, can retain significant residual autocorrelation. Extreme finite STAR forecast errors remain in accuracy scores; degenerate inferential statistics are reported as unavailable or uninformative, not as standard non-rejections.
- **Comparison scope:** Linear VAR does not identify causal macroeconomic transmission or rule out multivariate nonlinear dynamics; no historically release-by-release nowcast is reconstructed.
- **CPI AR(2) versus AR(12):** The lag-order robustness does not constitute an unperformed paired statistical comparison or an equivalence test.
- **Data construction:** The two October 2025 LOCF levels are author-processed research inputs, not official observations. Alternative interpolations and imputations were not tested, and the early-ending sensitivity sample cannot fully identify imputation effects.
